# CuePOS Pakistan Facebook and Instagram campaign

## Campaign position

CuePOS should enter the Pakistan market as a focused operating system for snooker clubs, not as a generic POS product. The campaign should speak to club owners and managers who want clearer table billing, a better view of daily operations, and a practical way to manage café sales, players, and reports.

## Recommended campaign setup

| Setting | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Campaign name | `CuePOS Pakistan — Snooker Club Leads` |
| Objective | Leads |
| Primary conversion location | Instant form, with a WhatsApp conversation variant after the first test |
| Market | Pakistan, with initial emphasis on Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, and Gujranwala |
| Language | English creative with simple Pakistan-market wording; test an Urdu-English version after the first creative read |
| Core audience | Club owners, operators, managers, and Accounts Center accounts showing interest in snooker, billiards, cue sports, sports clubs, hospitality, or small-business management |
| Age starting point | 23–55, then let campaign data guide expansion |
| Placements | Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Stories, and Reels; use the 4:5 master and 1:1 companion creative |
| Test budget | Start with a controlled 7-day test budget that the advertiser approves in Ads Manager; do not publish spend without account-owner confirmation |
| Success event | Completed lead form with club name, city, table count, and WhatsApp contact |

The first campaign should contain one broad Pakistan ad set and one interest-led ad set. Each ad set should have three creatives: the operations angle, the revenue-control angle, and the café-plus-club angle. This gives the delivery system distinct messages without fragmenting the campaign into too many small audiences.

## Instant form

Use the form title **Get a CuePOS club demo** and the intro **See how a Pakistani snooker club can manage tables, billing, players and café sales from one workspace.** Request the following fields: full name, club name, city, WhatsApp number, email address, and approximate table count. Keep the table-count question as a multiple-choice field with `1`, `2–5`, `6–10`, and `More than 10`.

The completion screen should say **Thanks — we’ll contact you about your club setup** and use the website or WhatsApp follow-up button. The sales response should qualify the club by table count because CuePOS pricing is table-based: Free for one table, Pro at Rs. 449 per table per month for up to 10 tables, and Premium at Rs. 400 per table per month for clubs with more than 10 tables.

## Visual creative briefs

### Creative A: 4:5 feed master

Create a premium Facebook and Instagram lead-ad visual for Pakistani snooker-club owners. Show a modern Pakistani snooker club interior with green baize tables, warm practical lighting, a club operator checking a tablet near the counter, and a subtle CuePOS dashboard interface visible on the device. Use CuePOS’s deep navy, emerald, and soft mint palette. Keep the upper-left area clean for a short headline and keep all text inside generous mobile-safe margins.

Render this exact headline: **Run your snooker club with CuePOS**. Render this exact support line: **Tables · Billing · Café · Reports**. Render this exact CTA label: **Start free with 1 table**. Add a small footer line: **Made for Pakistani snooker clubs**. Avoid fake logos, cluttered screens, unreadable dashboard details, casino imagery, or exaggerated financial claims.

### Creative B: 1:1 square companion

Create a clean square social ad for Pakistani snooker-club owners. Use a close, premium view of a green snooker table, cue, score pad, and a phone showing a simple live table status interface. Use a deep navy background with emerald accent light and one strong focal point. Keep the typography large and the composition simple for mobile feed scanning.

Render this exact headline: **Every table. One clear system.** Render this exact support line: **CuePOS for Pakistani snooker clubs**. Render this exact price callout: **Free for 1 table**. Render this exact CTA label: **Get a demo**. Avoid clutter, generic corporate stock imagery, and unsupported claims.

### Creative C: Reels/Stories vertical concept

Create a vertical 9:16 social ad visual for Pakistani snooker-club operators. Show a fast but premium sequence concept in one composed frame: an active table, a cashier counter, café items, and a clean phone interface representing CuePOS. Use a dark navy-to-emerald gradient with strong contrast and a large central text-safe area.

Render this exact headline: **Your floor. Under control.** Render this exact support line: **Live tables · Easy billing · Better records**. Render this exact CTA label: **Start your free workspace**. Avoid dense copy and keep all important text away from the top and bottom interface zones.

## Follow-up flow

The lead should receive a prompt response from the club sales team through WhatsApp or phone. The first reply should ask for the club’s city, table count, and preferred demo time. Leads with more than 10 tables should be routed to the Premium conversation; one-table leads should be offered the Free Plan first.

## Measurement

Use Meta’s exact metric names when reporting performance. Review Results, Reach, Impressions, Amount spent, Link clicks, and Cost per result at the campaign and ad-set level. For the business outcome, track completed lead forms and the percentage of leads that book a demo. Do not judge the first test on one day of data; compare the two ad sets and three creative angles across a complete test window.
