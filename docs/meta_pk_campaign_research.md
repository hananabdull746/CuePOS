# Meta campaign research for CuePOS Pakistan

Meta’s official leads-objective guidance says a Leads campaign can collect prospect information through instant forms, calls, or conversations in Instagram Direct, Messenger, and WhatsApp. For CuePOS, the recommended first conversion path is an instant form or WhatsApp conversation so a club owner can request a demo without needing a long website journey.

Meta’s official image requirements say advertisers should use the highest-resolution images available and link to the Ads Guide for full placement specifications. The official placement guidance surfaced Facebook Feed at 1:1 1080 × 1080 or 4:5 1440 × 1800 as recommended minimums. The creative set will therefore prioritize a 4:5 vertical feed master and a 1:1 square companion, with simple text hierarchy and safe margins for mobile placements.
